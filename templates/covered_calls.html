Covered Call

Covered calls only. Record fills from your broker and track the shares committed to each call.

Option cash flows and realized option P&L are tracked here separately from stock performance. Reference quote estimates appear on open calls and are excluded from stock totals. Tax adjustments are not included. Cash balances remain your manual snapshots.

Call prices

Look up standard call quotes before recording a broker fill. Quotes may be delayed and are not guaranteed execution prices.
